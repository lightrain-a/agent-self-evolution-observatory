## 3. Is this enough for a paper?

### Controlled causal mechanism branch

**Yes, if the V3 primary gate passes.**

The paper already has:

1. treatment-support/availability evidence;
2. a completed global causal comparison with an honest inconclusive result;
3. a prospective exact-same-pool crossed causal test;
4. a frozen simple-effect claim gate preventing post-outcome cherry-picking.

This is more scientifically substantial than merely running many benchmark/model cells because each layer answers a different causal question.

### Standalone top-conference strong-story branch

**Conditionally sufficient, not automatically complete.**

The decisive branches are:

- **V3 interaction FAIL:** experiment quantity is irrelevant; do not rescue with more models, more skeletons, or more benchmarks. Stop E2-R17 as a standalone strong paper and merge/pivot the interface/negative evidence.
- **V3 interaction PASS + secondary procedural 5/5 gate PASS:** controlled act/learn divergence is supported on the frozen suite. This is already a credible controlled causal paper. One natural transport experiment is the highest-value optional strengthening.
- **V3 interaction PASS + secondary procedural gate FAIL:** the workload is still large enough, but the scientific consequence is weaker. Do not add more synthetic replication. The one best claim-expansion experiment is natural/out-of-family transport with a positive future-skill simple-effect endpoint.

## 4. Why five skeletons are enough to proceed but not enough to overclaim

Five independent matched skeletons are the **low end** of confirmatory mechanism evidence. Their value comes from matched crossing and exact causal control, not large N.

Therefore:

- show all five `I_h` magnitudes;
- never convert R=4 into 20 independent mechanism units;
- do not claim population-level semantic generalization;
- do not add synthetic skeletons after seeing V3 outcomes to rescue a failed gate.

Adding more synthetic skeletons *before seeing any V3 outcome* could raise external confidence, but current independent reviews have repeatedly judged that no further pre-Stage-A redesign is justified. The information gain from one later natural transport test is higher than from doubling synthetic skeleton count.

## 5. What is NOT needed merely for workload appearance

Do not add these before the scientific branch warrants them:

- a benchmark zoo;
- several second/third backbones;
- more V3 replicates beyond R=4;
- more heldout tasks merely to inflate N;
- a generic failure-specificity arm;
- more synthetic skeleton families;
- a learned router just to make the paper look like a method paper.

A second backbone is robustness only after the central claim already survives. Failure-specificity is a mechanism decomposition only if the paper later chooses that stronger claim.
