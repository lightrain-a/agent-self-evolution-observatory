## A–M adjudication

A. **PASS — scientific unit.** Family is the independent scientific unit. Topology arms, UPDATE/NO_UPDATE/SHAM branches, and repeats are nested measurements and must not be counted as extra `n`.

B. **PASS — reserve and selection.** The 24-family reserve, pre-topology eligibility, frozen `N*`, stable-hash ordering, and reserve activation only for preregistered eligibility attrition avoid ordinary optional stopping and effect-dependent backfilling.

C. **PASS — repeat qualification.** A disjoint 4–6-family development panel may freeze `R* in {2,3}` for execution stability. These families remain excluded from confirmatory inference and repeats cannot be increased because a confirmatory result is borderline.

D. **PASS — target-only eligibility / post-treatment selection repair.** `TARGET_ONLY_VERIFICATION` is the key causal repair: positive repair uptake is measured on a topology-neutral surface before INDEPENDENT/LOW/HIGH outcomes. Once admitted, every family remains in every topology arm regardless of later target success. A panel-level target-retention falsifier is sufficient; no family-level post-treatment success filter may be reintroduced.

E. **PASS — same-update topology identification.** With the same pre-update snapshot and identical frozen REAL_REPAIR bytes, `UE_HIGH - UE_INDEPENDENT` is a valid within-family topology-moderation contrast provided the frozen topology arms differ in intended shared-state/prerequisite coupling rather than non-topological difficulty or obligations.

F. **PASS — SHAM subset.** A stable-hash eight-family SHAM subset on INDEPENDENT/HIGH is an efficient falsifier for generic persistent-text/context effects; a full-panel third branch is unnecessary.

G. **PASS — RQ3 zero-extra-call baselines.** Random, Same-App, resource-count, graph-distance, and ExposureRank may all use the same frozen held-out labels. The outcome-aware oracle remains an upper bound, not a normal baseline.

H. **PASS — prospective prediction.** Disjoint held-out families plus pre-frozen ExposureRank, `k`, tie-breaks, and ablations identify a prospective top-k enrichment claim. They do not create a second causal-topology theorem.

I. **PASS — GTCC stage.** Always Commit, Target-Only Validation, Random-k, Same-App-k, and GTCC form a sufficient budget-matched policy set. Full Non-target Check is correctly an oracle/upper-bound subset. Practical equivalence to cheaper controls stops GTCC novelty rather than triggering more samples.

J. **PASS — external baselines.** ACE/SkillOpt/SkillRevise need not enter the primary RQ1/RQ2 table because they alter repair-generation or acceptance semantics and would destroy the exact-same-update estimand. At most one compatible updater later is sufficient as a plug-in/generalization check.

K. **PASS — workload.** Default RQ1/RQ2 `N*=16, R*=2` is a reasonable minimum-effective design for a substantial within-family mechanism effect; N may be frozen prospectively up to 24 from outcome-independent precision criteria. SHAM, held-out prediction, conditional GTCC, and one optional secondary actor are proportionate. A seven-model sweep or extra seeds for appearance are not scientifically justified.

L. **PASS — residual novelty.** Generic forgetting/interference is insufficient novelty by itself. The residual contribution survives only through the ladder: verified positive local repair → previously satisfied collateral regression → exact-same-update topology-dependent externality → prospective structural prediction.

M. **PASS — stop discipline.** Gate 0/1, the RQ1→RQ2 dependency, held-out prediction stop, GTCC equivalence stop, and no-rescue rule prevent negative primary results from being converted into post-hoc family/model/baseline searches.
