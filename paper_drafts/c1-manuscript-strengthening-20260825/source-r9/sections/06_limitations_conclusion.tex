\section{Limitations}
The success/failure writer branch is a bundled protocol contrast, not a pure reward-bit intervention; decoding-seed/noise-floor replication is unavailable and two initial failure-label calls produced no text. Native exposure is source-item availability rather than identified treatment-residual consumption. Stage tests differ in units and power, so the first-action boundary is operational evidence localization, not causal mediation or proof that later policy surfaces cannot differ. Shopping and Reddit remain domain-limited, and other writers/backbones are untested.

The repair evidence has two different boundaries. Prompt hashes establish exact treatment \emph{delivery}, not internal cognitive compliance, and the simple explicit-use reminder is unsupported as an actionable repair. The later state-conditioned pilot uses 12 disjoint fresh states and shows a higher aggregate uptake mean, but its preregistered cross-state consistency gate fails: only 3/12 states improve over the memory-only adapter, four worsen, and three cells dominate 90.9\% of squared contrast mass. We therefore treat state binding as a heterogeneous proof of concept rather than a validated general repair. One state-conditioned binder output also violates the requested length format, although that state has zero uptake in all arms. Neither the old 23-state holdout nor the new 19-template holdout is consumed after these pilot results.

\section{Conclusion}
The same persistent update looks strong at write, active at source-item retrieval, weak at measured native action/outcome, and consequential under forced supply. The prerequisite class explains why observing fewer than $W/E/U/O/F$ aliases distinct diagnoses. Engineering probes then refine what the diagnosis means: a simple memory-use reminder does nothing, whereas a known-style state-conditioned binding adapter raises average first-action uptake on fresh states but only heterogeneously and fails the preregistered consistency gate. The practical rule is therefore two-stage: \textbf{credit only what the strongest supported stage justifies, then demand separate intervention evidence before granting repair authority}. State divergence is not behavioral authority; availability is not use; stage diagnosis can point to a repair family without making that repair universal.
