\section{Limitations}
The released success/failure branch jointly changes reflection instruction and outcome/reward semantics; decoding-seed binding and same-condition noise-floor replication are unavailable, two failure-label calls ended before assistant text, and source-faithful live WebArena navigation is unavailable. We therefore estimate a bundled writer-protocol contrast, not a pure reward-bit effect.

Stage tests differ in units, samples, power, and intervention surfaces, so first-action uptake is only an \emph{evidence-localization boundary}, not an identified latent attenuation onset or mediator. The 125/172 statistic is source-item retrieval, not treatment-residual exposure or policy use. The frozen logs do not identify which branch-differentiating content is actually consumed downstream. Forced injection bypasses native retrieval/context composition and measures supplied-evidence capacity.

Shopping is sparse (34/36 zero); Reddit is sparse, sign-heterogeneous (6/8 zero), and lacks matched exposure/action measurements, so neither a universal effect nor cross-domain action boundary is claimed. External validity is restricted to the frozen released writer/policy configuration and the Shopping+Reddit substrate evaluated here; other writers, backbones, domains, and memory architectures are untested. The CBRG extension stopped before behavioral evaluation; no behavioral repair efficacy is claimed.

\section{Conclusion}
Persistent self-improvement is a transport problem, not a single memory-score problem. On the frozen Shopping intervention, the released success/failure writer reliably creates different persistent states and native retrieval frequently exposes the corresponding source-memory item, yet the first structured action is the first measured native stage whose branch contrast is not supported. Terminal differences are sparser still. The positive forced fixed-evidence control shows that branch-specific memory content can influence the downstream system when supplied, so weak native transport should not be summarized as global downstream insensitivity.

The main methodological lesson is therefore to preserve stage identity. Write distance, retrieval exposure, policy uptake, and terminal outcome are different observables with different units and tests; they should not be divided into a post-hoc ``transport efficiency'' or collapsed into a binary claim that memory did or did not matter. Our ordinal boundary localizes where the frozen evidence first becomes unsupported after preceding prerequisites are supported or observed. It does not identify a latent causal attenuation onset, and source-item retrieval is not itself treatment-residual exposure or policy use.

For evaluations of self-improving agents, the practical rule is simple: do not grant a persistent update behavioral authority because its stored representation changed or because its carrier item appeared in context. Credit downstream control only when the relevant contrast survives into measured decisions or outcomes, and state explicitly when a capacity control bypasses part of the native path. This discipline reconciles the apparently conflicting write-only, retrieval-only, forced-only, and endpoint-only views of the same system without inventing a stronger causal claim than the evidence supports.
