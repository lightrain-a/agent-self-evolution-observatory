\section{Limitations}
The success/failure writer branch is a bundled protocol contrast, not a pure reward-bit intervention; decoding-seed/noise-floor replication is unavailable and two initial failure-label calls produced no text. Native exposure is source-item availability rather than identified treatment-residual consumption. Stage tests differ in units and power, so the first-action boundary is operational evidence localization, not causal mediation or proof that later policy surfaces cannot differ. Shopping and Reddit remain domain-limited, and other writers/backbones are untested.

The repair pilot has the opposite boundary: prompt hashes establish exact treatment \emph{delivery}, not internal cognitive compliance. Its negative result shows only that the tested simple explicit memory-use-check realization is not supported as an actionable repair under the frozen pilot gate. We do not tune stronger variants on those 13 states or consume the sealed 23-state holdout for the same treatment family after observing the pilot.

\section{Conclusion}
The same persistent update looks strong at write, active at source-item retrieval, weak at measured native action/outcome, and consequential under forced supply. The prerequisite class explains why observing fewer than $W/E/U/O/F$ aliases distinct diagnoses. But diagnosis is not a prescription: a prospective, fully completed memory-use-check pilot fails to selectively increase branch-specific uptake and therefore stops before confirmatory scaling. The practical rule is two-stage: \textbf{credit only what the strongest supported stage justifies, and require a separate falsifiable intervention before granting repair authority}. State divergence is not behavioral authority; availability is not use; diagnosis is not repair.
