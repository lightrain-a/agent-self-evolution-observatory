\section{Related Work}
\paragraph{Self-evolving memory and memory management.} ReasoningBank distills reusable reasoning from self-judged successful and failed experience~\citep{ouyang2026reasoningbank}; \citet{xiong2026memorymanagement} study experience following, error propagation, and quality-aware addition/deletion. These are not our novelty. C1 instead creates two persistent states from the \emph{same} source trajectory and follows that controlled contrast through later system surfaces.

\paragraph{Retrieval, utilization, and our residual.} Mem2ActBench evaluates active memory application rather than factual recall~\citep{shen2026mem2actbench}; MemCoRL co-optimizes retrieval and utilization~\citep{liu2026memcorl}; and Chain-of-Memory targets the retrieval-recall/reasoning-accuracy gap~\citep{xu2026chainofmemory}. Hence ``retrieval is not use'' and generic utilization optimization are not our novelty. Our residual is the controlled transport signature---write, native exposure, first-action uptake, outcome, and forced capacity---and its diagnostic consequence: bound update credit by the supported stage and prospectively test the next repair surface.
