\section{Limitations}
The financial AgentDojo audit remains the source of the 0.931-versus-0.647 provenance association and the ten persistent W$\rightarrow$W cases. Its public release does not currently expose the per-query ReasoningBank artifacts needed for an exact matched swap. A reconstruction preflight recovered the benchmark and ReasoningBank implementation, while the execution environment lacks auditable access to the exact executor and embedding models used by the financial study. An exact financial replication therefore remains support and experiment debt. These access constraints carry no scientific authority.

Our executed intervention is a controlled bridge on released WebArena browser-state evidence. It tests the same-query memory channel without reproducing the financial environment. Four of six outcome-blind candidate pairs survive the frozen information-equivalence gate. Their embedding cosines are all above 0.90, so the 0.75 cosine floor is not the fragile part of qualification. A post-hoc robustness audit exposes a more important identification issue. The original verifier accepts all four primary pairs under the strict guidance-equivalence criterion, while a blinded DeepSeek reviewer accepts none and a blinded Kimi reviewer accepts only one. Table~\ref{tab:eq-robustness} shows the pair-level disagreement. No primary pair receives unanimous strict approval across the three reviewers. Pair membership and the R4 statistic remain frozen; we do not remove disagreeing pairs after outcomes. The audit therefore leaves the causal hypothesis active while showing that R4 does not robustly isolate provenance from residual differences in actionable memory content.

The clean controlled swap produces a +0.1667 mean terminal-success difference in the predicted direction. The effect exceeds the preregistered 0.15 magnitude floor. Its one-sided 100,000-permutation value is $p=0.0785$, above the frozen 0.05 support threshold. The result therefore remains inconclusive under the preregistered decision rule. No equivalence margin was frozen, so this outcome is not evidence of zero provenance effect. The counterevidence gate also remains closed. A post-hoc design audit shows why this four-pair experiment had limited decision power: with six rollouts per condition and a 0.15 planning effect, representative Bernoulli baselines give only about 0.28--0.35 power at four independent pairs and require roughly 18--22 independent eligible pairs for 80\% power. We therefore forbid extending seeds on the same four pairs as a significance rescue. A confirmatory controlled swap must use an independently frozen support set.

The reported financial provenance accuracies are rounded to three decimals. The integer correct counts used in our decomposition are the unique counts consistent with the reported support sizes and rounded accuracies. The transition allocation beyond the ten W$\rightarrow$W cases is therefore derived from public aggregate statistics.

The earlier explicit-metadata bridge held generic guidance byte-identical while changing only a structured SUCCESS or FAILURE source label. Across 144 requested calls it produced a 0.000 terminal-success difference. That bridge had no preregistered equivalence margin and manipulates metadata instead of naturally generated ReasoningBank memories, so it does not close the causal question. We also rescanned the frozen WebArena release under the same structural qualification used for R2, excluding every C01 task already exposed by prior experiments. No fresh read-only, multi-reference, deterministic string-match task with complete released evidence remains. Reusing the R4 pairs or relaxing qualification after observing $p=0.0785$ would therefore be outcome-driven rescue. Replication beyond financial agents and WebArena remains debt for a broad cross-domain claim.

\section{Conclusion}
Self-evolving memory carries process history. The released financial-agent audit shows a large accuracy gap by memory provenance and concentrates every reported persistent W$\rightarrow$W failure in failure-derived retrievals. ReasoningBank makes provenance an upstream intervention because successful trajectories and failed trajectories are written through different reflection objectives.

We operationalize that causal question with a pre-outcome information-equivalence gate and an executed matched-memory swap. The controlled effect is +16.7 percentage points in the predicted direction across four frozen pairs, while the frozen permutation test does not cross the support threshold. The subsequent blinded verifier audit shows that semantic matching itself is not robust enough to identify provenance beyond content in this realization. The causal claim therefore remains active and experimentally sharpened, with two explicit confirmation requirements: a larger independently frozen pair population and a robust multi-reviewer information-equivalence gate. Persistent memory systems should preserve how an item was learned and test whether that provenance changes future reuse outcomes before treating semantically similar memories as interchangeable.
