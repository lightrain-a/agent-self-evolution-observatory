\subsection{Matched-pair acceptance contract}
A proposed provenance pair must satisfy four checks. The future task is identical under both interventions. The two memory items must support the same procedural objective. One member must come from a successful acquisition trajectory and the other from a failed acquisition trajectory. A verifier must find no extra task-solving fact in either memory. Pairs that fail any check are excluded before outcomes are observed.
