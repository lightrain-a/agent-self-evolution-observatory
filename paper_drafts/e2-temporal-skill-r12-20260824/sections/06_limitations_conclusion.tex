\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}
The unavailable TimeSage-EV evaluated snapshot prevents an exact rerun; our BEA/NOAA/EIA replay is independent mechanistic evidence. Its 35 endpoints are uneven: EIA is cutoff-focused, same-domain grounding transfer has two held-out endpoints, Kimi alignment has three, and cross-domain grounding is null. DeepSeek is primary, Kimi is a provenance-clean replication, the mini model is exploratory, and EIA is post-review validation frozen before outcomes. Repeats measure robustness rather than task sample size. These limits rule out broad cross-domain or model-invariant claims.

Two stronger identification layers remain open. N is the original agent rather than a resource-matched neutral-helper arm, and G is target-blind but not guaranteed behaviorally neutral; a prospectively validated G$_0$ is required to test value beyond neutral extra computation. No operation-matched retrieval-side treatment was executed, so callable-container value is also unresolved. Appendix~A freezes both future treatments; invocation/order/length/date audits do not replace their missing outcomes.

\section{Conclusion}
Reusable temporal procedures can be binding repairs in specific non-ceiling model--task regimes. Requiring T to beat both the original agent and matched target-blind assistance turns one apparent +28-point cutoff effect into no repair, while other grounding, cutoff, and alignment cells remain positive. The lesson is narrower than ``skills help'': persistent operations should be promoted only against explicit controls, with neutral-compute and retrieval-container value treated as separate estimands.
