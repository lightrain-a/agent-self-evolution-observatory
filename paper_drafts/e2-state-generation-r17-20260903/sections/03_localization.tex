\label{sec:localization}

\paragraph{Evidence selection alone is unresolved.}
The closed study contains 48 matched update pairs, 96 learned states, and 1728 heldout units. WIN-C exposes the served winner; MRW uses the deterministic failed-nonwinner projection under the frozen mixed-pool rule while preserving the same acting winner. Mean MRW$-$WIN-C is $0.02315$ (SD $0.07797$); the preregistered one-sided sign-flip test gives $p=.171875$ and the bootstrap interval crosses zero. The immutable verdict is \texttt{HOLD\_MRW\_UNDERPOWERED\_OR\_HETEROGENEOUS}. This rejects a universal rejected-witness story while preserving heterogeneity as a fact to explain.

\paragraph{A richer witness selector does not rescue the story.}
On one explicitly development-only stream, Progress-Fail and Progress-Contrast each reached 14/18, below the historical First-Fail state at 17/18; the frozen continuation gate therefore stopped. We retain this failed intervention rather than tuning a witness score after outcomes.

\paragraph{The strong state survives when the updater is removed.}
We froze the historical First-Fail state and a matched WIN-C state and re-evaluated without state generation. Across two fresh replicates, First-Fail achieved 15/18 versus 14/18 and 16/18 versus 12/18. This is selected-case evidence, but it shows that the useful behavior can persist once the state itself is held fixed.

\paragraph{Byte-identical evidence does not regenerate the strong state.}
We reconstructed the exact learner-visible evidence that produced the strong development state; all 16 rendered packet hashes matched the originals. Two fresh paired updater realizations then yielded 15/18 versus 15/18 and 11/18 versus 12/18, rather than the historical +4/18 contrast. We therefore record a frozen \emph{state-generation replication failure}. With only two fresh realizations this is not a population variance estimate. It is a localization result: evidence bytes are insufficient to characterize the treatment consumed by the future actor, so state generation becomes an explicit experimental variable. The full machine-readable verdict identifier is preserved in Appendix~\ref{app:integrity}.

The supportable claim at this stage is deliberately local: on a selected development case, a useful persistent state remained useful when frozen, whereas byte-identical evidence failed to reproduce the historical effect through fresh free-form synthesis. This motivates, but does not prove, a state-generation bottleneck.
