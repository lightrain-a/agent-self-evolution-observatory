\section{Limitations and Conclusion}

The strongest current localization is deliberately a selected development case. Two fresh exact-evidence updater realizations can falsify deterministic regeneration of the historical state, but they cannot estimate a population variance component. The typed vocabulary is also engineered for the current spreadsheet substrate and may align with its evaluator. Our score-only generic falsifier and fresh suite address this alternative only within the controlled domain. Finally, the compiler differs from free-form synthesis in determinism, canonicalization, scope, redundancy, and state length, so a future positive bridge result is a complete state-generation-method effect rather than an isolated determinism effect. Hosted temperature-zero calls may also retain residual nondeterminism; outcome-conditioned redraws are therefore forbidden rather than averaged away.

The scientific lesson is nevertheless concrete: experience reaches future behavior only through a persistent artifact. In our development chain, a global rejected-witness intervention is heterogeneous, a useful state survives when frozen, and byte-identical evidence does not reliably regenerate its historical effect through fresh free-form synthesis. The evidence and the state are therefore distinct experimental objects. Separating evidence selection, state generation, state semantics, and downstream behavior provides a sharper diagnosis of self-evolution failures and a falsifiable route toward variance-controlled persistent-state generation.
