\section{Experiments}
\label{sec:experiments}

Our controlled substrate uses the first-party MindMemOS SkillEvolver and deterministic SpreadsheetBench-style tasks~\citep{liang2026mindmemos}. Search pools, evidence renders, learned states, verifier inputs, and provider budgets are content-addressed; completion audits precede score access. Exact runtime/model bindings are deferred to Appendix~\ref{app:integrity}.

\subsection{M2: State-Semantic Sufficiency}

Before evaluating an automatic compiler, we ask whether the candidate persistent-state semantics are sufficient locally. On the selected development stream we freeze: G0 (initial skill), G1 (output reload/verification), G2 (Inspect$\rightarrow$Read$\rightarrow$Compute$\rightarrow$Write$\rightarrow$Save$\rightarrow$Reload/Verify), and G3 (G2 plus clean tool-error recovery). Each is evaluated on the same 18 development heldout tasks. The primary gate selects the simplest G1$<$G2$<$G3 arm exceeding G0 by at least $1/18$; a pre-outcome 17-task provider-era sensitivity can veto but never rescue a PASS.

At this revision, 45/72 state-task units are validly complete and the remaining 27 are frozen for exactly-once recovery after a hosted quota reset. No partial scores have been opened. If M2 passes, only G0 and the simplest passing arm receive exactly two complete frozen-state remeasurements; failure stops the branch before automatic method evaluation.

All cells start from the same $S_0$ and frozen search pools; learning cannot alter the served acting winner. Winner exposes the served winner package on all eight update pools. First-Fail-4 deterministically replaces exactly four eligible mixed-pool winner packages with the lowest-index failed nonwinner and leaves the other four unchanged. This equalizes replacement count, not information content.

\paragraph{Fresh evaluation.}
The bridge uses a fresh b7--b9 suite with zero task-ID and XLSX-hash overlap with the earlier controlled suite and no use of untouched E3 b5/b6. Its 120 formal tasks comprise 96 update tasks (12 streams$\times$8), 12 SCREEN heldout tasks, and 12 disjoint VALIDATION heldout tasks. Six family-balanced streams are frozen for each stage. Suite construction and collision audits are Appendix~\ref{app:integrity}.

For stream utility $J$, the primary contrasts are
\begin{align}
G_F &= J(\mathrm{FF4\_COMP})-J(\mathrm{FF4\_FREE}), \\
S_C &= J(\mathrm{FF4\_COMP})-J(\mathrm{W\_COMP}), \\
I &= G_F-[J(\mathrm{W\_COMP})-J(\mathrm{W\_FREE})].
\end{align}
SCREEN passes only if mean $G_F>0$ and $S_C>0$, each is positive on at least 4/6 streams, mean $I>0$, FF4\_COMP exceeds \texttt{SCORE\_ONLY\_GENERIC\_MAX}, and no parity/technical violation occurs. Otherwise VALIDATION remains sealed. If SCREEN passes, the unchanged method runs on the disjoint VALIDATION streams/panel under the same directional conditions. No threshold, primitive, compiler rule, or replacement dose can be changed between stages.

\paragraph{Decision logic.}
A failed generator contrast stops the state-generation method story. A positive generator contrast with failed source contrast supports at most reliable state compilation, not rejected-failure learning. A failed score-only falsifier removes trajectory-conditioned diagnosis. Only survival of generator, source, falsifier, and interaction gates makes a separate untouched-E3 proposal eligible; it does not automatically authorize E3.
